# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GLiFS guide to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, or background.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them get oriented.
- Accepting feedback gracefully and giving it with care.
- Keeping the focus on building the glif language together.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any form of discrimination.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [@tuteth_](https://x.com/tuteth_) on X.
Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove content, warn, or ban
contributors who violate these standards. Reports will be handled discreetly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
